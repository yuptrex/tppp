Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

TextPro ("the App", "we") is a messaging app that lets you chat, call, and share media with people you connect with. This policy explains what information we collect, how it's used, and the choices you have. By creating an account you agree to this policy and our Terms of Service.

Information we collect

Account infoEmail address and password, handled by Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your raw password ourselves.
Profile infoDisplay name, username, bio, and avatar image you choose to provide.
AgeYour birth year and an age-verified flag, collected at signup to confirm you meet the minimum age requirement. We do not store your full date of birth.
Messages & mediaText, photos, voice messages, and files you send are stored to deliver them, then removed according to our retention rules.
Device infoA push-notification token, used only to deliver notifications to your device.
Camera & microphoneAccessed only when you actively use camera capture, voice messages, or calling features — and only while you're using them.

How we use information

We use your information to create and secure your account, deliver messages and calls between you and people you chat with, show you push notifications, and maintain basic app functionality — for example, showing your online status to people you message. We do not sell your personal information.

AI features

TextPro includes an optional AI Assistant you can chat with. When you use it, the messages, photos, and voice messages you send to the AI Assistant are transmitted to Google's Gemini API (Google Generative Language API) for processing, so it can generate a reply, describe an image, or respond to a voice message. This only happens for content you send directly to the AI Assistant — not your other conversations.

Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service in addition to this policy. Do not send information to the AI Assistant that you would not want processed by a third-party AI provider.

Voice & video calls

Calls are connected peer-to-peer where possible. A signaling message — who is calling whom, and connection setup data — passes through our servers to establish the call, but call audio and video are not stored or recorded by us.

Data retention & deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings inside the app, which removes your profile, messages, and account data from our systems. Some data may persist briefly in backups before final deletion.

Third-party services

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, real-time data storage, and hosting our backend. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API (Google Generative Language API) — powers the optional AI Assistant feature, as described above.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your profile information, block or report other users, disable notifications in your device settings, and delete your account and data at any time — either from within the app, or from our web deletion page.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
